# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and do not publish another person's private information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, and sexualized language or imagery. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports promptly and protect reporter privacy to the extent possible.
